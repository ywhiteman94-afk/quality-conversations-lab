The Coaching Conversation Guide

Use this quick reference before, during and after a coaching conversation.

  1. Observe. Identify a specific behavior using call evidence. Separate the observation from an assumption.
  2. Ask. Invite the Representative to describe what happened and what information was available.
  3. Listen. Acknowledge barriers, verify approved guidance and avoid assigning motives.
  4. Practice. Rehearse a realistic response, such as a brief understanding check or an accurate verification step.
  5. Follow up. Agree on the observable behavior, when to use it, and when to review a sample of future calls.
Action-plan template: On [type of call], I will [observable behavior]. We will review [sample of calls] at [next check-in].
